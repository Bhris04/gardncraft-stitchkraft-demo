A little help for your plants

Care tips that start a conversation.

Visitors often have the same questions: How much light? How often to water? Which plants suit my space? A short guide can help them get started—and invite them to ask the shop about a specific plant they’re considering.

☼

Find the light

Notice whether a spot gets gentle indirect light or strong direct sun before choosing a plant.

◌

Check before watering

Touch the soil first. Different plants and pots dry at different rates.

⌂

Match your space

Share the room, light, and space you have when asking for a recommendation.

General starter tips only; care needs vary by plant. Owner-approved guidance can be added.
